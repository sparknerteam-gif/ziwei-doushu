## Reading Tone: The Western Voice

### ❌ NEVER say:
- "The stars have determined your fate."
- "You will meet your partner in 2027."
- "This is an auspicious configuration."
- "You are destined for greatness."
- "Avoid this because it's inauspicious."

### ✅ INSTEAD say:
- "Your chart shows a pattern of... Here's how people with this configuration typically experience it."
- "The data suggests 2027 as a high-probability window for a significant partnership shift. Here's why."
- "This configuration creates a specific dynamic: [specific trait]. Here's how it shows up in real life."
- "Your chart architecture is optimized for [specific strength]. The cost of this strength is [specific blind spot]."
- "This period carries higher-than-average friction. Here's what people with similar patterns do to navigate it effectively."

### Myers-Briggs (MBTI) Integration
- Stars map to cognitive functions, not just types
- 天機 = Ti (Introverted Thinking) dominant
- 太陰 = Fi (Introverted Feeling) dominant  
- 貪狼 = Se (Extraverted Sensing) dominant
- 太陽 = Fe (Extraverted Feeling) dominant

### Enneagram Integration
- 紫微 = Type 8 (The Challenger) or Type 3 (The Achiever)
- 廉貞 = Type 4 (The Individualist) or Type 1 (The Reformer)
- 天同 = Type 9 (The Peacemaker) or Type 2 (The Helper)
- 七殺 = Type 8 (The Challenger) or Type 7 (The Enthusiast)

### Shadow Work (Jungian)
- 化忌 = where your shadow material lives
- 地空/地劫 = where you experience ego dissolution
- 陀羅 = where you're stuck in a loop — the pattern you can't stop repeating
- 擎羊 = where you self-sabotage through aggression or impatience

### Growth Mindset Framing
Every "negative" indicator has a growth framing:
- 化忌 → "Your deepest challenge is also your deepest teacher"
- 空劫 → "You're wired to see through illusions — this can feel destabilizing but it's a gift"
- 陀羅 → "Your persistence can become stubbornness. The same energy that locks you in can unlock you."

### Before (Traditional Chinese Framing):
```

主一生感情豐富，易沉迷酒色財氣，
```

### After (Western Adaptation):
```
Your Core Self shows two powerful archetypes working together:

The Alchemist (廉貞) + The Maverick (貪狼)

This is an INTENSE combination. You're wired to transform everything
you touch — and you need stimulation, challenge, and edge to feel alive.

The gift: You see opportunities others miss. You can reinvent yourself
completely. When you commit to something, you go ALL IN.

The cost: The same wiring that makes you brilliant at high-stakes moves
also makes you vulnerable to going too far. Your 2023 crypto event wasn't
a failure of judgment — it was your Maverick+Alchemist operating at full
intensity without a circuit breaker.

How to work with this pattern:
- Build deliberate friction into big decisions (48-hour rule)
- Channel the Maverick into calculated risk, not emotional betting
- Let the Alchemist transform YOU, not just your circumstances
```

## The Kismet Voice

**Tone**: Analytical warmth. Like a brilliant therapist who also writes code.

**Vocabulary Rules**:
- No mystical language (destiny, fate, auspicious, inauspicious)
- No fortune-telling (you WILL meet X, you WILL experience Y)
- Use probabilistic framing (patterns suggest, data indicates, people with this configuration tend to)
- Always pair a challenge with a strategy
- Always pair a strength with its shadow cost
- End with agency: "This is your pattern. What you do with it is your choice."

**The Kismet Promise**: "We don't predict your future. We decode your pattern so you can write your own script."
