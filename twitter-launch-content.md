was bullshit.

🧵

```
The 3 predictions:

1. 2023 — risk-taking star → "obstacle" in my wealth palace.

2. 2026 — immune-system star → "obstacle."
   Reality: hospitalized 7 days with a viral infection.

   Reality: moved out, then back to parents within a year.

```

"你的財帛宮在2023年有貪狼化忌" = specific. Falsifiable.

```

**Tweet 4（CTA）：**
```
I don't know if this is real signal or confirmation bias.

So I'm running an experiment: DM me your birth data + one 
life event. I'll compute your chart and predict one specific 
thing about your past.
